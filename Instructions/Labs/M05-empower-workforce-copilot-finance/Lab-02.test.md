## Scenario

### Your Role

You are a **Financial Analyst at Fabrikam, Inc.**, a global manufacturer of sustainable consumer products. Your primary responsibility is to help leadership make informed business decisions by providing timely, data-driven financial analysis.

### Fabrikam's Two Major Initiatives

Fabrikam is currently focused on two strategic priorities:

**Initiative 1: Launching the EcoSmart Product Line**
- Fabrikam is introducing a new line of sustainable consumer products called **EcoSmart**.
- The Finance team is responsible for validating **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)** estimates to ensure pricing and margin targets are achievable.
- Leadership needs to understand which product features or materials are driving higher production costs so they can optimize suppliers or production processes.

**Initiative 2: Evaluating the Potential Acquisition of Relecloud**
- Fabrikam's executive team is evaluating whether to acquire **Relecloud, Ltd.**, a technology company that would expand Fabrikam's digital product portfolio.
- The Finance team needs to analyze Relecloud's business case, model acquisition scenarios, and present findings to leadership.
- Decisions around valuation multiples, synergy savings, and integration planning all depend on accurate financial modeling.

### Your Finance Manager

Your direct manager is **Robin Kline**, Fabrikam's Finance Manager. Throughout Exercise 1, Robin will assign you specific analysis tasks and expect polished, data-driven outputs you can present to leadership.

## Prompting Reminder

> 💡 **Tip:** The Introduction unit in this module reminded you of the four key elements of an effective prompt: **Goal**, **Context**, **Sources**, and **Expectations**. Keep these elements in mind as you create prompts in every task of this exercise.

Before starting Lab-03, take a moment to review this framework:

- **Goal** — What do you want Copilot to do?
- **Context** — Why are you asking? What is the business situation?
- **Sources** — Which documents, spreadsheets, or data should Copilot use?
- **Expectations** — How should the response be formatted or presented?

A well-formed prompt that includes all four elements will consistently produce more actionable, detailed, and accurate results than a vague, high-level request.
