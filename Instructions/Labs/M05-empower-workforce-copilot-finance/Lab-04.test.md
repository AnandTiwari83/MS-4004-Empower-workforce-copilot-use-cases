# Lab-04: Exercise 1, Task 2 — Use Copilot in Teams to Summarize Meeting Notes

## Lab Overview

In this lab, you use **Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat in Teams** to process meeting notes from a Finance department discussion and turn them into actionable outputs. You will extract key decisions and action items, draft a task list for meeting participants, generate a meeting timeline, and produce a policy brief — all using Copilot to save time and ensure nothing falls through the cracks.

**Estimated Duration:** 15 minutes  
**Difficulty Level:** 200 (Intermediate)

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lab, you will be able to:

- Use **Copilot Chat in Teams** to summarize and analyze meeting notes from an uploaded text file.
- Extract key decisions, action items, and responsibilities from unstructured meeting content.
- Generate downloadable summaries, task lists, and calendar invites using Copilot prompts.
- Draft a policy brief based on a workflow discussed during a meeting.

---

## Prerequisites

- Completion of **Lab-01** (resource files uploaded to OneDrive).
- Completion of **Lab-03** (Excel COGS analysis) is recommended for context, but not required.
- Access to Microsoft Teams for the web.

## Background and Scenario

### What Happened in the Meeting

As a Financial Analyst at **Fabrikam, Inc.**, you recently attended a meeting with:

- **Robin Kline** — Finance Manager
- **Amari Rivera**, **Quincy Brooks**, **Miguel Reyes**, **Eric Solomon** — Finance department members

The purpose of the meeting was to discuss the **upcoming year-end financial analysis and reporting requirements**, including how new data fields and automation might affect financial reporting timelines and formats.

Unfortunately, you did not have time to take detailed notes during the meeting. Now you need Copilot to help you reconstruct the key outcomes from the meeting notes file.

### Why a Text File Instead of Live Teams?

> 📝 **Note:** This lab uses a bring-your-own-subscription (BYOS) model, which means there is no pre-configured Microsoft 365 tenant for Fabrikam. To replicate the experience of a real Teams meeting, meeting notes are provided in a **.txt file**. In a corporate environment, these conversations would typically be stored in Teams channels or meeting transcripts. The text file preserves sender names, timestamps, and message content, allowing you to practice exactly as you would with real Teams data.

## Task Instructions

### Step 1: Download and Upload the Meeting Notes File

1. Select the following link to download the source file:  
   [**Fabrikam Finance meeting notes.txt**](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?linkid=2347810)

2. Save the file to your **OneDrive** account so it is accessible to Copilot in Teams.

---

### Step 2: Open Copilot Chat in Teams

1. In Microsoft Edge, navigate to the **Microsoft 365 home page**: `https://www.microsoft365.com`

2. Select **Apps** in the left navigation pane, then select **Teams**.

3. In Teams for the web, select **Copilot** in the left navigation pane.

4. The **Welcome to Copilot Chat** page will open.

> ✅ **Checkpoint:** You should see the Copilot Chat interface with a prompt field ready for input.

---

### Step 3: Summarize Key Decisions, Updates, and Action Items

Your first goal is to get a high-level summary of the meeting that covers what was decided, what updates were shared, and what each person needs to do next.

**Write and submit your own prompt** asking Copilot to:
- Summarize the **key decisions**, **updates**, and **action items** in the attached **Fabrikam Finance meeting notes.txt** file.
- Attach the file to your prompt.

> 💡 **Guidance:** Use the four-element framework (Goal, Context, Sources, Expectations) when writing your prompt. Your goal is a meeting summary; your source is the .txt file you just uploaded; your expectation might be bullet-point format organized by category.

**After Copilot responds:**
- Review the summary to verify it covers decisions, next steps, and individual responsibilities.

> ✅ **Expected Outcome:** A structured summary showing key meeting decisions, updates, and action items organized by topic or participant.

---

### Step 4: Generate a Downloadable Summary Report

You need to distribute this summary to the meeting participants.

**Write and submit your own prompt** asking Copilot to:
- Generate a **downloadable file** containing the meeting summary.

**After Copilot responds:**
- Select the download option to save the generated document.
- Review the downloaded file to confirm it is suitable for distribution.

> ✅ **Expected Outcome:** A downloadable document file (e.g., Word or PDF format) containing the meeting summary.

---

### Step 5: Create a Task List with Priorities and Deadlines

Your Finance Manager expects clear accountability for each action item that came out of the meeting. You need a task list that assigns work to the right people with realistic priorities.

**Write and submit your own prompt** asking Copilot to:
- Generate a **detailed task list** for each meeting participant based on the action items in the notes.
- Assign **deadlines or priorities** to each task (for example, "high priority for compliance checks").

> 💡 **Guidance:** Be specific in your prompt about wanting per-participant tasks with priority levels. Copilot can infer participant names and responsibilities from the meeting notes.

**After Copilot responds:**
- Review the task list to verify that each participant has been assigned relevant tasks with clear priorities.

> ✅ **Expected Outcome:** A structured task list showing each meeting participant's responsibilities, with priority levels and suggested deadlines.

---

### Step 6: Draft a Team Email Containing the Task List

Once you have the task list, you need to communicate it to the team.

**Write and submit your own prompt** asking Copilot to:
- Draft an **email** to the Finance team that includes the task list.
- Use a **professional tone** appropriate for internal Finance communications.

**After Copilot responds:**
- Review the draft email.
- Note: In a real-world scenario, you would copy and paste this into an Outlook email. For now, simply review the draft and proceed.

> ✅ **Expected Outcome:** A well-structured draft email ready for distribution to the Finance team, with the task list embedded in the body.

---

### Step 7: Generate a Timeline of Key Milestones

With multiple action items, deadlines, and reporting requirements coming out of this meeting, leadership will want to see a consolidated view of milestones.

**Write and submit your own prompt** asking Copilot to:
- Generate a **timeline** of the key milestones discussed in the meeting.

**After Copilot responds:**
- Review the timeline to confirm it reflects the major deliverables and reporting dates from the meeting.

> ✅ **Expected Outcome:** A chronological timeline showing key milestones, deadlines, and dependencies discussed in the Finance meeting.

---

### Step 8: Draft a Calendar Invite for the Team's Next Meeting

The Finance team agreed to reconvene to review progress. Use Copilot to draft a meeting invitation.

**Write and submit your own prompt** asking Copilot to:
- Draft a **calendar invite** for the team to reconvene for a follow-up meeting.

**After Copilot responds:**
- Review the output. Note that Copilot may respond in different ways:
  - It may suggest **several date options** for you to choose from.
  - It may draft the **invitation message** and ask you to specify a date.
- If Copilot asks for a date, provide one in a follow-up prompt.
- In a real-world scenario, you would send this invite directly from Copilot. For this exercise, review the draft and proceed to the next step.

> ✅ **Expected Outcome:** A draft calendar invitation with a meeting subject, attendees, agenda summary, and proposed date/time.

---

### Step 9: Draft a Policy Brief on the Override Approval Workflow

During the meeting, the team discussed a specific process for handling overrides, exceptions, and compliance-related tasks. You want to create an authoritative reference document that all team members can follow.

**Write and submit your own prompt** asking Copilot to:
- Draft a **short policy brief** explaining the **override approval workflow** that was discussed during the meeting.
- Frame it as an authoritative guide for handling exceptions, overrides, or compliance-related tasks.
- Ensure it is written clearly enough that all team members can follow the same process, reducing ambiguity and risk.

**After Copilot responds:**
- Review the policy brief to confirm it captures the workflow accurately and is suitable for use as a team reference document.

> ✅ **Expected Outcome:** A concise policy brief (1–2 pages equivalent) describing the override approval process, including steps, approvers, and escalation paths.
