# Lab-09: Exercise 2, Task 2 — Use Copilot in PowerPoint to Create an Executive Presentation

## Lab Overview

In this lab, you use **Microsoft Copilot in PowerPoint** to generate a Finance leadership presentation from the **Smart Sensor contract comparison** document you created in Lab-08. You will use Copilot to build a complete slide deck, add a risk mitigation slide, create a Vendor Advantage Summary slide, and close with an Action Plan slide — all without manually creating slides from scratch.

**Difficulty Level:** 100 (Beginner)

- Create a polished executive presentation that includes topic slides, a risk mitigation slide, a vendor comparison summary, and an action plan.

## Background and Scenario

### Why a Presentation?

The **Smart Sensor contract comparison** document you built in Lab-08 is comprehensive and analytical — ideal for a detailed review. However, Finance leadership meetings require visual clarity and brevity. Your task is to transform the key findings from that document into a concise, visually engaging presentation that leadership can consume quickly and act upon.

You plan to use Copilot in PowerPoint to generate the deck automatically, then refine it by adding slides that address leadership's likely questions about risk mitigation, vendor advantages, and next steps.

---

## Understanding Copilot in PowerPoint

PowerPoint provides two ways to interact with Copilot:

### Standard Copilot Prompts (Chat Mode)
Use this mode when you want to **draft slides quickly, summarize content, or generate speaker notes** without restructuring the deck.

### Edit with Copilot
Use this mode when you want Copilot to **work directly in the presentation** — reorganizing slides, refining slide text, improving layouts, or making iterative edits across multiple slides. Copilot previews changes and applies them directly once you confirm.

> 💡 **This task uses the Edit with Copilot functionality.**

## Task Instructions

### Step 1: Open PowerPoint and Create a Blank Presentation

1. In Microsoft Edge, navigate to the **Microsoft 365 home page**: `https://www.microsoft365.com`

2. Select **Apps** → **PowerPoint**.

3. In PowerPoint for the web, select **Blank presentation** to create a new, empty file.

---

### Step 2: Enable Copilot and Attach the Source Document

1. Select the **Home** tab in the ribbon (if not already visible).

2. At the right end of the **Home** tab ribbon, select **Copilot**.

3. In the Copilot pane, select the **(+) sign** in the prompt field and then select **Add work content** from the drop-down menu.

4. Attach the **Smart Sensor contract comparison** document from your OneDrive.

5. Verify that the **Edit with Copilot** icon (✏️) is visible in the prompt field. If not, select **(+)** → **Edit with Copilot**.

> ✅ **Checkpoint:** The Smart Sensor contract comparison file is attached, and the Edit with Copilot icon is active.

---

### Step 3: Generate the Initial Slide Deck

Write and submit your own prompt that instructs Copilot to create a slide presentation. Your prompt should incorporate the four key elements and address the following requirements:

- **Source**: Based on the attached **Smart Sensor contract comparison** document.
- **Audience**: Targeted to the **Finance leadership team**.
- **Structure**: Include a **separate slide for each key finding topic area**, such as:
  - Pricing
  - Delivery Timelines and Terms
  - Warranties and Support
  - (And any other topics covered in your document)
- **Content per slide**: Each slide should **compare the contract features for Adatum Corporation and Contoso, Ltd.** for the selected area.
- **Visuals**: Use visuals to keep the presentation engaging.
- **Formatting**: Use bullet points on slides for clarity.

> 💡 **Guidance:** Write this as a comprehensive prompt that tells Copilot both what to create and how. The more specific you are about slide structure, audience, and formatting, the better the initial deck will be.

**After submitting your prompt:**
- Copilot may ask a series of questions about the presentation (e.g., template selection, style preferences). Answer any questions based on your preferences, or select **Skip all** to let Copilot use its best judgment.
- Wait while Copilot generates the slides. This may take several minutes.

> 💡 **Note on Copilot behavior:** Copilot sometimes generates slides automatically with no further confirmation. Other times, it presents an outline and asks how to proceed — in that case, tell it to proceed with the outline. Either way, generating the full deck typically takes several minutes.

**After Copilot completes the slide generation:**
- Review all slides to assess completeness and quality.

> ✅ **Expected Outcome:** A multi-slide presentation covering each major topic area from your contract comparison document, with comparison content for both vendors on each slide.

---

### Step 4: Add a Risk Mitigation Slide

After reviewing the deck, you anticipate that Finance leadership will ask about risk mitigation — which is not yet addressed in the presentation.

1. **Review the current slides** and identify the most appropriate position in the deck to insert a risk mitigation slide.

2. In the **slide pane on the left**, select the position where you want Copilot to insert the new slide (click between two slides to indicate the insertion point).

3. **Write and submit your own prompt** asking Copilot to add a slide that:
   - Contains the **top recommendations for mitigating the risks** identified in the contract comparison.
   - Includes **visual emphasis** for critical points, such as callouts or highlights.

> 💡 **Note:** Copilot may ask additional questions (such as which template to use for the new slide). Follow any prompts and wait while the slide is generated.

**After Copilot responds:**
- Review the new risk mitigation slide.

> ✅ **Expected Outcome:** A new slide inserted at your chosen position that clearly presents risk mitigation recommendations with visual emphasis on the most critical points.

---

### Step 5: Add a Vendor Advantage Summary Slide

You want a slide that gives leadership a quick-reference summary of which vendor excels in which areas.

1. **Locate your cursor** at the end of the current deck (after the last existing slide).

2. **Write and submit your own prompt** asking Copilot to add a **Vendor Advantage Summary** slide at the end of the deck that:
   - Indicates which vendor offers **better terms for each topic area** covered in the presentation.

**After Copilot responds:**
- Review the Vendor Advantage Summary slide.

> ✅ **Expected Outcome:** A summary slide at the end of the deck showing a vendor-by-vendor comparison of strengths across all topic areas covered in the presentation.

---

### Step 6: Add a Final Action Plan Slide

The last slide should give leadership clear direction on next steps following the meeting.

1. **Select your cursor** in the slide deck after the Vendor Advantage Summary slide (this should be the final position in the deck).

2. **Write and submit your own prompt** asking Copilot to add an **Action Plan** slide as the final slide that:
   - Identifies the **next steps for negotiations and risk mitigation**.
   - Includes a **timeline** and **responsible roles** for each action.

> 💡 **Note:** If Copilot generates the Action Plan slide without a timeline, you can ask Copilot to create a separate **Next Steps Timeline** slide, then manually move the timeline content into the Action Plan slide if desired.

**After Copilot responds:**
- Review the Action Plan slide to confirm it includes timeline and ownership information.

> ✅ **Expected Outcome:** A final Action Plan slide that outlines negotiation and risk mitigation next steps, with a timeline and responsible roles clearly identified.

---

### Step 7: Save the Presentation to OneDrive

1. Select **File** → **Save as** (or use the file name area above the ribbon) to save the presentation.

2. Save the file to your **OneDrive** account with the name:  
   **Smart Sensor contract comparison - Finance**

> ⚠️ **Important:** Save the file with this exact name to OneDrive. You will use it as the source for your **Loop workspace** in Lab-10.

> ✅ **Checkpoint:** The file **Smart Sensor contract comparison - Finance.pptx** is saved in your OneDrive.
