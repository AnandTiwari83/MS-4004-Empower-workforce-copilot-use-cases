## Understanding Copilot's Email Drafting Workflow

When you use Copilot in Outlook to draft an email, each new request generates a **new draft version**. You can:
- **Navigate between drafts** using the back and forward arrows in the Copilot pane.
- **Request modifications** to the currently displayed draft.
- **Select "Keep it"** when you are satisfied with a draft to apply it to the email.

> 💡 **Tip:** By default, each new Copilot request modifies the most recent draft. If you want to revise an older draft, navigate back to it first.

## Task Instructions

### Step 1: Open Outlook and Start a New Email

1. In Microsoft Edge, navigate to the **Microsoft 365 home page**: `https://www.microsoft365.com`

2. Select **Apps** in the left navigation pane, then select **Outlook**.

3. In Outlook on the web, select **New mail** (or **+ New email**) to create a new message.

> **📸 Screenshot placeholder:** Capture the Outlook on the web compose window immediately after opening a new email.  
> Suggested filename: `M05-Lab11-Step01-OutlookNewEmail.png`

> **🔧 Troubleshooting:**
> - If the Copilot icon is not visible in the email body, look for a sparkle (✨) icon in the compose toolbar, or select **More options** (···) in the compose bar.
> - If Copilot does not include your Loop link in the email, add it explicitly by pasting the URL directly into the email body after selecting "Keep it".
> - If the **Change Tone** option is not visible, scroll down in the Copilot menu — it may be at the bottom of the available options list.
> - If you accidentally close the Copilot draft window, you can reopen it by selecting the Copilot icon in the email compose area again.
> - If the Loop link is not clickable in the sent email, verify the URL was pasted correctly and starts with https://.

### Step 2: Open Copilot and Draft the First Version of the Email

1. In the body of the new email, select the **Open Copilot** icon (it may appear as a Copilot icon or sparkle icon in the message body toolbar).

> **📸 Screenshot placeholder:** Capture the email compose area with the Copilot icon visible in the message body toolbar.  
> Suggested filename: `M05-Lab11-Step02-CopilotIconInEmailBody.png`

2. In the Copilot prompt field that appears, **write and submit your own prompt** asking Copilot to draft an email that:
   - Is addressed to your **Finance colleagues**.
   - **Requests feedback** on your draft contract comparison presentation.
   - **Includes the Loop workspace link** (paste your link from Lab-10 into the prompt) for collaboration.
   - Asks Copilot to **include this link** in the email body for Finance colleagues to use.
   - Uses a **professional, formal tone**.
   - Conveys **urgency** — feedback is needed before the Finance leadership meeting.

> 💡 **Guidance:** Paste the actual Loop workspace URL into your prompt so Copilot can reference it. Your prompt should specify the recipient group, the purpose, the link to include, the tone, and the urgency.

**After Copilot responds:**
- Review **Draft 1** of the email.
- Note the overall structure, tone, and content.

> ✅ **Expected Outcome:** A professional, formally toned email (Draft 1 of 2+) requesting feedback on the contract comparison presentation with the Loop link included.

### Step 3: Revise to a More Conversational and Collaborative Tone (Draft 2)

After reviewing Draft 1, you decide that a more conversational approach might resonate better with your colleagues and encourage participation.

**Write and submit a follow-up prompt** asking Copilot to:
- Revise the email to make it **more conversational and friendly in tone**.
- **Start by highlighting the benefits of using the Loop component** for real-time collaboration.
- **Explain in greater detail why feedback is important** for this presentation.
- **Outline the next steps** clearly for the recipient.

**After Copilot responds:**
- Review **Draft 2**.
- Notice how this version differs from Draft 1 in tone, opening, and structure.
- Use the **back arrow** to view Draft 1 again if you want to compare the two versions side by side.
- Select the **forward arrow** to return to Draft 2.

> ✅ **Expected Outcome:** A second draft (Draft 2 of 2) with a more conversational tone, emphasizing collaboration benefits and explaining the importance of feedback in more detail.

> **📸 Screenshot placeholder:** Capture the second Copilot-generated email draft with the more conversational tone.  
> Suggested filename: `M05-Lab11-Step04-Draft2ConversationalTone.png`

### Step 4: Restructure to Bullet Format with Deadline Emphasis (Draft 3)

You like the content of Draft 2, but you feel that a more structured format would make it easier for busy colleagues to quickly grasp what you need.

**Write and submit a follow-up prompt** asking Copilot to:
- **Rewrite the email** with the key review points presented in **bullet format**.
- **Start the email** by stressing the **deadline for feedback** clearly and prominently.

**After Copilot responds:**
- Review **Draft 3**.
- Assess whether the bullet format and deadline emphasis make the email more immediately actionable.

> ✅ **Expected Outcome:** A third draft (Draft 3 of 3) that opens with the feedback deadline and organizes key review points in a scannable bullet list.

> **📸 Screenshot placeholder:** Capture the third draft showing the bullet-based structure and deadline emphasis.

### Step 5: Add a Motivating Closing Paragraph (Draft 4)

The structure of Draft 3 is strong, but you want to end the email on a motivating note that conveys the value of your colleagues' contributions.

**Write and submit a follow-up prompt** asking Copilot to:
- Add a **single closing paragraph** at the end of the email that:
  - Explains how your colleagues' input will **improve the final deliverable**.
  - Includes a **motivating phrase** such as: *"Your insights will make a big impact."*

**After Copilot responds:**
- Review the updated email with the new closing paragraph.

> ✅ **Expected Outcome:** The email now ends with a closing paragraph that motivates collaboration and acknowledges the value of the team's feedback.

### Step 6: Adjust the Tone

After rereading the latest draft, you want to fine-tune the overall tone one more time before finalizing.

1. In the Copilot menu, scroll down and select **Change Tone**.

> **📸 Screenshot placeholder:** Capture the Copilot **Change Tone** menu with the available tone options visible.  
> Suggested filename: `M05-Lab11-Step07-ToneChangeMenu.png`

2. Select one of the available tone options from the menu to see how Copilot adjusts the email.

3. Review the new draft that Copilot generates.

4. If you are still not satisfied, **write a direct tone request** asking Copilot to change the tone to be:
   - **Friendly and confident**, but **still professional**.

**After Copilot responds:**
- Review the final draft.

> ✅ **Expected Outcome:** A polished email with a friendly, confident, and professional tone — ready to send to your Finance colleagues.

---

### Step 7: Finalize and (Optionally) Send the Email

1. When you are satisfied with the email, select **Keep it** in the Copilot window.  
   This takes you out of Copilot draft mode and into the actual email.

2. **Add the recipient(s)** in the **To** field. You can send it to your personal email address to verify the output.

3. **Add a subject line** that clearly indicates the purpose (e.g., "Feedback Requested: Smart Sensor Contract Comparison Presentation").

4. (Optional) Select **Send** to send the email.

5. If you sent the email to yourself, open it in your inbox and verify:
   - The Loop workspace link is clickable and works correctly.
   - The email reads professionally and conveys the right tone and urgency.

> ✅ **Expected Outcome:** A finalized, professional email sent (or ready to send) to Finance colleagues, requesting timely feedback on the contract comparison presentation with a working Loop collaboration link.
